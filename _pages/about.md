Hello! I'm a Ph.D. student at [MIT's Operations Research Center](https://orc.mit.edu/), advised by Dr. Retsef Levi. My research combines deep learning, generative AI, and optimization under uncertainty to design novel data-driven decision-making tools for large-scale industry applications.

Currently, I am designing novel multimodal predictive models and a generative AI-enabled framework to support daily capacity management decisions and improve patient flow at [Massachusetts General Hospital](https://www.massgeneralbrigham.org/en/patient-care/services-and-specialties/locations/massachusetts-general-hospital), in partnership with its Healthcare Systems Engineering team. My research is supported by the MGH-MIT Doctoral Fellowship.

In the summer of 2026, I was an R&D graduate student intern at [Sandia National Laboratories](https://www.sandia.gov/), where I developed multifidelity approaches to uncertainty quantification for large-scale stochastic programs. I implemented these methods in the open-source [SPAROW](https://github.com/sandialabs/sparow) software package, available to researchers and practitioners alike!

Before MIT, I received my Bachelor’s Degree from Rice University, with a double major in Operations Research (School of Engineering) and Mathematical Economics (School of Social Sciences).
